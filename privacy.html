Privacy, in plain words

What this site keeps about you, where it lives, for how long, who can see it, and how you delete all of it yourself.

Policy version 2026-11-19. When this page changes in a way that matters, the version changes and you are asked to agree again at your next login.

Where it is kept

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and private file storage; the project runs in Mumbai, India) holds everything in the table above. Your selfie sits in a private storage bucket that only the owner and admins can open.
  • The face server (Render) receives each picture only to compute the 512 numbers and check that it is a real, live face. It does not keep the picture.
  • When you use an AI feature (tutor, AI interviewer, planner), the text it needs (your question or answer and the lesson's text) is sent to Google's Gemini API, without your name or email.
  • Microphone (Interview Day, only when you press Record): the recording and the live transcript stay in your browser's memory and are never uploaded or saved; only a few numbers (pace, filler words, key points covered) are kept in your results. The live transcript uses your browser's own speech service (Chrome and Edge send the audio to the browser maker for this); other browsers show no transcript.
  • DSA Lab, explain your approach (only when you press Record): your recording stays in the tab's memory and is never uploaded. In Chrome the live transcript is made by the browser's speech service, which may send the audio to Google. Only counts (words, pace, fillers, key points) are saved with your study progress; the transcript is dropped once scored. Your notes are saved on this device only. A share link contains your code inside the link itself; nothing is uploaded.
  • Emails are sent through a mail delivery service that works for the course owner. It receives your email address, your first name and the mail itself: fixed sentences, a few counts (for example how many sections you finished this week) and links to this site. It never receives your selfie, your face data, your answers or the reason for a decision.
  • Your browser keeps a copy of your progress and, for 7 days, the lessons you opened, so they work offline. Logging out deletes them from that browser.

Who can see it

The course owner and the admins they appoint see your name, email, selfie, course access and study progress, to approve accounts and to help you learn. Other learners never see your name, progress or face; they only see anonymous counts of interview reports. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Emails

There are two kinds. Messages about your account (your account or a course you asked for was approved or not approved) are part of the service and are always sent. Study emails (a weekly summary of your own progress, and a reminder the course owner may send when you have not studied for a while, at most one a week) are off until you turn them on, here or on the home page. Every study email has a link that turns them off again, without logging in.

Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time. It removes, for good: your account and password, your selfie, your face template, your face-check and login logs, your course access, your synced progress, your interview reports, your job tracker, your AI usage counts, your error reports, the list of emails sent to you and your email choice. AI tutor questions are not linked to you, so they stay until their 180 days are up. The owner's private database backups (kept off the site, newest 14 days) still hold a copy until they roll off. The owner account itself cannot be deleted here.

Checking whether you are logged in…

Cannot log in any more (for example your face no longer passes)? Ask the course owner (the person who approved your account) to delete it for you: the admin page has the same Delete button.

Questions and changes

Questions about your data go to the course owner. If this page changes, the date above changes and you are asked to agree again before your next lesson.
